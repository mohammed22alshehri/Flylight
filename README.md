# Fly Light Logistics

Marketing and investment website for Fly Light Logistics, a logistics and shipping startup.

## What it does

An Arabic-language, right-to-left, single-page site that introduces the company and lets visitors become investors by contributing funds in exchange for company shares.

## Pages and sections

The home section introduces the company with a hero area and key stats such as the price per share. A contribute section lets an investor submit a contribution, upload proof of a bank transfer, and view the company's payment details. An admin dashboard, protected by a password, lets an administrator review submitted contributions and approve or reject them.

## Tech stack

HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, organized as a small design system with shared tokens, reusable components, and page-specific styles. Supabase is used as the backend for storing and managing contribution data. Deployed via GitHub Pages.
